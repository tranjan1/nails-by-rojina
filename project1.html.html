Important Guidelines

Studio Policies

30% Booking Deposit

A 30% non-refundable deposit is required to confirm all slot reservations. This amount is deducted directly from your total bill on appointment day.

15-Minute Grace Period

We offer a 15-minute grace window for late arrivals. Beyond 15 minutes, your appointment may need to be shortened or rescheduled to respect the next client.

24-Hour Notice

Please provide at least 24 hours' notice if you need to cancel or reschedule your slot so we can accommodate clients on our waiting list.

Lash Infill Policy

Infills are accepted depending on lash health and remaining extension count (at least 40% remaining). Foreign infills may require safe removal and a fresh set.
